# Stage 8 first-launch content preparation

Reusable preparation sheet. This checklist does not authorize formal import, Publish, Index or launch. Record the proposed first-launch scope and outstanding Owner decisions before requesting those actions.

- [ ] List intended first-launch Products, Categories, Applications, Fabric Library entries, Content and Home/About placements; nominate one primary indexable page for each primary search intent and confirm the English `/` URL namespaces.
- [ ] For each proposed real Product, record the verified sample/product/internal code/supply specification or explicit specification combination and evidence custodian. Report missing evidence as `Waiting for Real Product Data Validation`.
- [ ] Keep the minimum Draft requirement unchanged: name, one Primary Category and one image. Leave unknown facts empty; do not create empty public headings/tables or infer specifications, MOQ, capacity or certifications.
- [ ] Prepare originals and image roles through the existing Upload/Asset pipeline; confirm scan/processing and source/rights evidence where applicable. Source Declaration remains optional and OFF by default. Effective rights, approved revisions and live relationship eligibility remain authoritative; a copied file does not grant public use.
- [ ] Prepare Company Facts for each factual corporate claim. Use `CloudWave Textile`; verify partner/owned-facility boundaries and do not infer factory ownership from CWT photography. Unknown history, staff, equipment, customers and contact details stay empty.
- [ ] Recheck Product/Taxonomy/Application/Fabric Library boundaries and derived eligibility. Keep Draft, Review, Publish and Index separate; AI creates drafts only. Ordinary filters/search and low-value Library entries remain noindex.
- [ ] Prepare human review assignments and the controlled formal-import batch scope. Private Inquiry files never become public Assets or AI knowledge automatically. No formal data is imported during checklist preparation.
- [ ] Before a future launch decision, collect formal media/rights and Company Fact review, SEO/Canonical/Sitemap/mobile/accessibility results, complete accepted Migration Fresh/Upgrade evidence, independent backup/restore acceptance and Owner-approved RPO/RTO.

Open decisions: first-launch content scope and timing; verified business contact details; named human reviewers; acceptable database/site loss windows and recovery duration. Formal import, Publish/Index and launch each retain their existing authorization boundary.
