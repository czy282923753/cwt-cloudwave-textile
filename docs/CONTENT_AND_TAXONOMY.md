# Content, taxonomy, and search-intent governance

## Product taxonomy dimensions

1. Material / Fiber
2. Structure / Construction
3. Commercial Collection
4. Surface / Hand Feel

Applications are a separate end-use system. A product has one Primary Category from the product taxonomy and may have additional terms and Applications.

Sportswear is an Application. Sports Fabric may be a Commercial Collection or curated commercial landing page. Keyword-to-page mapping determines which page owns the primary intent; duplicate Sports Fabric and Sportswear pages are forbidden.

## Editorial channels

- Fabric Knowledge: material knowledge, comparison, selection, and terminology.
- China Textile Guide: Chinese textile industry, clusters, markets, and supply chain.
- China Sourcing Guide: supplier selection, sourcing workflow, MOQ/sample concepts, and commercial decisions.

## Topic clusters

SEO Topics connect pillar pages, cluster articles, products, taxonomy pages, Applications, and internal links. Topic membership does not create pages automatically.

## Keyword-to-page process

Collect evidence → cluster semantic intent → classify intent → nominate one owner route → check overlap → create or improve content → review internal links → publish → evaluate aggregated search and inquiry data.

AI may suggest topics and links but cannot create, publish, or index pages automatically.

## Company and partner facts

Claims about CWT history, facilities, teams, certifications, manufacturing, quality control, and partner factories must reference a verified Company Fact. Partner factories are never described as CWT-owned without verified evidence and approval.
