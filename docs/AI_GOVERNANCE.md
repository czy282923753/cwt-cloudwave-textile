# AI governance — frozen boundary

Phase 1A does not implement AI assistants or a knowledge base.

Future AI may create reviewable drafts for descriptions, features, tags, Applications, FAQ, metadata, topic ideas, and internal links from supplied real information. It may not infer technical facts, company facts, permissions, factory ownership, certifications, customer claims, or publish/index content.

Customer inquiry files never automatically enter public assets, knowledge, prompts, training data, or model context. All future AI work must retain evidence, human review, and auditability.
