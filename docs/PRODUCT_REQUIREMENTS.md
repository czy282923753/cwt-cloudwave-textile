# CWT product requirements — frozen V1.1

## Positioning

CloudWave Textile is a Guangzhou-based multi-category fabric supplier and textile sourcing partner. The platform is a B2B SEO acquisition, knowledge, inquiry, and operations system—not a retail store, generic brochure site, or single-factory site.

Brand positioning: **Professional Fabric Supplier & Textile Sourcing Partner in China**.

Homepage H1: **Professional Fabric Supplier in China**. Supporting copy communicates sourcing-partner capabilities without implying that all goods come from CWT-owned factories.

## Commercial loop

Google search → category/product/application/content page → professional trust → Find Your Fabric Solution → description or image inquiry → CRM follow-up → quote/sample/deal outcome → data informs SEO and product operations.

The north-star outcome is qualified organic inquiries and commercial value, not raw page count or traffic.

## Audiences

Overseas brands, garment manufacturers, sportswear and yoga businesses, fabric importers and wholesalers, trading companies, cross-border sellers, and China sourcing professionals. Initial market hypotheses include Southeast Asia, Turkey and nearby markets, Europe, and North America; real performance data determines later priorities.

## Product rules

- An indexable product page represents a real product, sample, code, supply specification, or explicit specification combination.
- Keyword-swapped duplicate products are forbidden.
- A draft requires name, Primary Category, and one image.
- Technical facts may be empty and are never inferred by AI.
- Products may have multiple taxonomy terms and Applications but one Primary Category.
- Empty fields do not render.
- Product Code, Supplier Type, internal tags/notes, price, and MOQ are hidden by default.

## Content and discovery

The platform includes Products, Applications, Fabric Library, Fabric Knowledge, China Textile Guide, and China Sourcing Guide. Fabric Library Entry, Product, and Asset are separate entities. Search and ordinary filtered pages are noindex. Thin Fabric Library entries are noindex.

## Inquiry and CRM

Name and email are required. A description or at least one image is required; country and WhatsApp are optional. Contacts can have many inquiries and an optional lightweight Organization. Pipeline states are New, Reviewing, Qualified, Quoted, Sample, Negotiation, Won, Lost, Spam, and Archived.

## AI boundary

AI may suggest drafts for copy, features, tags, Applications, FAQ, metadata, and internal links. It cannot infer composition, weight, width, construction, MOQ, factory ownership, certifications, or other facts. AI cannot publish or enable index.

## Explicit Phase 1A exclusions

Excel import, 30–50 real-product expansion, ZIP/Word/PDF/TXT ingestion, AI assistants, knowledge base, SEO Assistant, analytics dashboards, formal multilingual pages, market pages, AI customer service, marketing automation, advanced Fabric Library filtering, complex Organizations, AI microservices, production deployment, DNS changes, and unapproved external pushes.
